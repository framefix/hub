GOOD TO KNOW

A few quick answers.

Will you change the room or its contents?+

Enhancement focuses on clarity, light, and color while aiming to preserve your space and its contents. Full Listing Polish and Multi-Property Pass also include sky enhancement or replacement when needed. Tell us which details must stay unchanged; other content changes can be discussed separately.

Can a very blurry photo be fixed?+

It depends on the original. Some photos can be improved more than others, and fine detail may need to be reconstructed. We’ll review what you send and discuss what is realistic.

How much does it cost?+

Essential Refresh is $49 for up to 5 photos. Full Listing Polish is $99 for up to 15 photos. Multi-Property Pass is $249 for up to 3 properties and 45 photos. These are flat fees; we’ll confirm any work outside your chosen package before starting.

How soon will I receive my photos?+

Essential Refresh includes 24-hour delivery. Full Listing Polish includes priority 24-hour delivery. Multi-Property Pass includes dedicated priority processing with a 12–24 hour turnaround. Send your original photos and requirements so we can confirm the delivery timing.

Is this an instant online photo editor?+

FrameFix is a photo enhancement service. The comparison above shows our example work. This website helps you explore the service and prepare an enquiry; it doesn’t automatically edit your uploads.
